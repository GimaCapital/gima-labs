## 1. Gima RPV-20. Rug-Proof Vault Standard (BSC Testnet)

**Problem.** Token launches on BSC routinely end in rug pulls, and retail users have no reliable way to verify whether a contract is safe before committing funds.

**What I built.** A token standard and companion DApp designed to make malicious behavior structurally difficult rather than merely discouraged. The standard combines non-bypassable access control with programmable vault mechanics and publicly verifiable on-chain trust signals.

- **RPV-20 standard:** custom token standard with enforced supply and transfer rules
- **Trust Vaults:** time-locked custody with configurable release conditions
- **On-chain Trust Badges:** verifiable credentials that signal audited and compliant contracts
- **GimaDAO governance:** community-controlled parameter changes and upgrade approvals
- **Security model:** non-bypassable access control, vault time-locks, on-chain trust verification

**Stack:** Solidity, Hardhat, EVM (BSC Testnet), React, Node.js, PostgreSQL, Render

**Live demo:** https://gima-dapp-frontend.onrender.com

## 2. TheSpark. Savings Habit Platform

**Problem.** Low-income Nigerians face irregular income and limited access to structured savings products, which makes consistent saving difficult.

**What I built.** A consumer fintech product that turns saving into a short, achievable habit loop. Users commit to a 21-day cycle, track progress in real time, and graduate to higher tiers as their consistency improves.

- **21-day savings cycles:** short, repeatable commitment structure designed for habit formation
- **Real-time progress dashboard:** visual tracking of streaks, balances, and milestones
- **Tiered subscriptions:** progressive access tied to consistency and savings volume
- **Realtime data layer:** Firestore-backed state with live sync across sessions

**Stack:** Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Node.js, Firebase, Firestore, Render

**Live product:** https://thespark.cash
